EQ Archives

Research EverQuest in ChatGPT.

Connect ChatGPT to the historical websites, mailing lists and newsgroups in EQ Archives. Ask a research question, read the preserved evidence and follow citations back to the original sources.

Connect the archive

The connector is available as a custom MCP connection. It has not yet been listed in ChatGPT’s Plugins Directory. Your ChatGPT account or workspace must allow custom connections and developer mode.

  1. In ChatGPT, open Settings → Security and login → Developer mode and enable it, if available.
  2. Open Plugins, choose the plus button and name the connection EQ Archives.
  3. Use this MCP server URL and choose No authentication. No EQ Archives account or API key is required.

https://search.eqarchives.org/mcp

Start a new chat and select EQ Archives from the tools menu. For a longer investigation, select it as a source in Deep Research when that option is available in your account. See OpenAI’s current connection instructions if the controls in your account differ.

Try a research question

  • What did players report about camping the Ancient Cyclops for Journeyman’s Boots? Compare several historical accounts and cite the sources.
  • Find contemporary discussions of the Plane of Fear. Separate first-hand reports from later recollections.
  • Research early wizard strategies for quad kiting. Quote short passages and link to the archived discussions.

Read the evidence carefully

The connector searches the collection and retrieves extracted source text. It cannot edit the archive or access private accounts. Archive capture dates can differ from original publication dates. Estimated dates and image transcriptions generated by a model are labelled; check them against the source. The collection and text extraction are incomplete.

What the connection sends

ChatGPT sends the search queries and document IDs it chooses for the research task to EQ Archives. The connector does not request your full conversation, name, email or ChatGPT credentials. Query embeddings are generated on the EQ Archives server. Up to 128 queries and their embeddings are cached in process memory for reuse for five minutes; the oldest entries are evicted when the cache fills, and a restart clears it.

The connector does not write query bodies to application logs. Infrastructure may record operational information such as IP addresses, timestamps and errors. ChatGPT’s own data settings and policies also apply. Use public research topics and avoid including private information.
