    One persistent connection, no fixed request delay and unlimited bandwidth. Wayback errors trigger bounded retry backoff. The queue has no count limit. The automatic worker starts one site at a time; Undo stays on waiting Queue entries until capture starts. New captures inventory all file types without a file-count cap. Transport or storage limits pause the capture and retain its checkpoint; explicit Resume can extend exhausted transport allowances. Older batches retain their saved cumulative limits. Approved captures request all available dated versions from 1 January 1999 through 31 December 2006. Sites with 1999–2001 captures have discovery priority. Individual forbidden or missing files are recorded as coverage gaps while other files continue.
