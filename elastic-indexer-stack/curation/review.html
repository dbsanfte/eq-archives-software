Capture limits & dates

Capture batches use one connection, a 3-second delay and 128 KiB/s. Limits: 5 sites, 20 new URLs per site, 100 HTML files and 64 MiB. Prefer 1999–2001; use 2002–2007 when an exact URL has no first-tier capture.
